**MCS candidate, University of Wasit — I build Arabic-first developer tools and local-first software.**

## What I care about

- **Arabic-first interfaces** — RTL correctness, BiDi text, and Arabic typography inside generated PDFs, not as a later patch.
- **Local-first architecture** — offline queues, on-device indexes, deterministic exports. Your data stays on your device.
- **Agent-operated tooling** — software a non-programmer drives by talking, rather than a CLI they have to memorise.
